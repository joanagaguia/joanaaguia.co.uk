Assess Risks and Needs, Probation Digital

Seeing an entire assessment system for the first time

Assess Risks and Needs is one of MoJ Digital’s priority programmes, reframing how offender assessments are done. I led UX in a team of more than 30 agile, policy and operations colleagues.

Role
Senior interaction designer
When
January 2024 to March 2025
Worked with
Policy experts, user researchers, analysts, and product teams across probation and prisons
Skills used
  • Service blueprinting
  • Stakeholder management

The challenge

Our team’s remit was framed around replacing one assessment step. But user interviews showed that assessments of risk and needs happen again and again throughout someone’s time in prison and on probation.

Nobody could see that whole picture. Each team understood its own part, so the real opportunities for change were hidden between them.

What I did

I mapped the journey from the user’s point of view: which services they use, who they deal with, and what data is collected and why. I checked the existing processes with policy experts, to understand why each one existed.

Then I ran interviews and collaborative sessions with business analysts and product managers across the other product teams, to see how their services connected to ours. The result was a service blueprint covering every assessment at every stage of the custodial journey.

Alongside the blueprint, I:

  • Led workshops with digital, policy and operational colleagues
  • Prototyped complex, accessible services in code and Figma
  • Defined the roadmap for a private beta and coached less experienced designers

The outcome

A re-prioritised roadmap across 9+ teams.

For the first time, the programme could see how assessment data was duplicated across the whole journey. Our product manager redesigned the roadmap to prioritise work that removes that duplication.

The blueprint is still in use. My successor keeps it updated and continues to find new uses for it.
